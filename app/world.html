Method & pre-registration

Fixed before looking

The hypothesis, sample, metrics, quality rules and statistical test were written down and committed before any global value was extracted (research/global/PREREGISTRATION.md, commit 43f2fbb).

  • Population: … NISAR VALIDATED repeat pairs with HH (same track, frame, CRID), 21 Oct – 11 Nov 2026; |lat| ≤ 60°.
  • Sample: 200 pairs, simple random, seed 2026.
  • NISAR: 10 × 10 km window at the frame centre; ΔHH = 10·log₁₀(mean γ⁰ after / mean γ⁰ before) over jointly valid pixels.
  • SMAP: L3 Enhanced 9 km soil moisture, overpass nearest the NISAR local time, recommended-quality only.
  • Test: Spearman ρ, two-sided permutation p (10,000); Theil–Sen slope with bootstrap 95 % CI.
