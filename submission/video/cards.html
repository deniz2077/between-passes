Rain fools radar.

Between Passes shows what really changed.

Satellites find floods, harvests and cut-down forests by comparing two pictures. After rain, whole regions look "changed" when nothing happened. Between Passes sorts out those false alarms.

Between Passes · deniz2077.github.io/between-passes

  • A filter that sorts out rain's false alarms in NASA-ISRO NISAR radar change maps, using NASA SMAP soil moisture and NASA POWER rain.
  • For disaster teams looking for floods, agencies watching farms and forests, and farmers checking their fields.
  • 148 places in 39 countries, and one command for any place NISAR has flown.
  • Rules written before the data: tested worldwide twice, predictions locked before NASA released the data.
  • A screening filter: it says where to look, not what happened there.
Data: NASA-ISRO NISAR L2 GCOV VALIDATED · NASA SMAP · NASA HLS · NASA POWER · © OpenStreetMap contributors · Natural Earth
