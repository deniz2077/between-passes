Between Passes

A radar evidence lab built on NASA-ISRO NISAR data: what changed in the fields of Ceyhan, Adana between two radar passes — and what can honestly be concluded.

What we don't claim

  • The cause of any single parcel's change — only which explanations are consistent.
  • HV polarization — an unexplained gradient and interference flag; shown, not interpreted.
  • Field-level rainfall — NASA POWER is a coarse estimate. One case study, not an alert system.
  • The global test is site-scale (10 km windows), one 12-day interval, one random sample.
